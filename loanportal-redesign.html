Color is never the only signal.

Status badges pair a dot and a text label with the hue. Errors add an icon and a message. Rejected is shown as a tonal badge, not a solid red pill, so one rejected row does not overpower a table.

Alerts and toasts

Alerts stay in the page until the problem is fixed. Toasts confirm an action and leave on their own after five seconds, or on hover-out. Both say what happened and what to do next.

You can return to this application from the dashboard any time in the next 30 days.

Dialogs

Every dialog in the portal, on one template: title, one sentence of context, then actions with the safest choice first. Open any of them as a real modal. Focus is trapped, Esc closes it, and focus returns to the button.

Empty, loading and error states

An empty screen invites the next action. An error says what happened and offers a way out.

App bar, tabs and menus

The current page is marked with a fill and aria-current. Menus open on click and close with Esc or an outside click.
